<Note>
  TL;DR and FAQ extras are a **Premium** feature. On the Free and Basic plans, your articles are personalized without them. See [Plans & Pricing](/plans/plans-and-pricing).
</Note>

On the Premium plan, BrandVoice Pro can add two optional sections to your content: a TL;DR summary and an FAQ. Both are designed to help your content get picked up by search engines and AI search tools like Perplexity and ChatGPT.

## What the extras do

- **TL;DR.** A short summary of the piece, useful for readers who skim and for search engines that surface concise answers.
- **FAQ.** A set of questions and answers structured around conversational, search-intent phrasing, which is the kind of content that earns featured snippets and shows up in AI search answers.

## Turning them on

With a Premium plan, you can enable the extras two ways:

- **Per profile.** Set a default so every piece for that brand includes them.
- **Per article.** Toggle them on or off for an individual generation.
