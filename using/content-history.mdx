BrandVoice Pro keeps a history of everything you generate, so nothing you create is lost and you can always pick up where you left off. Your history lives in the sidebar and is saved to your account, available from any device.

## How it works

- **Automatic saving.** Your generated articles and social posts are saved to History for you — there is nothing to remember to click. Work is captured as you move between steps or to another screen.
- **Browse and preview.** Open the History tab in the sidebar to see your past generations, newest first, and preview any of them.
- **Restore in one click.** Select an earlier piece to load it back into the workspace, ready to copy, tweak, or build on.

<Note>
  Because saving is automatic, you can move from personalizing an article to generating social posts — or step away entirely — without losing your work.
</Note>

## Managing your history

You can remove an item from your history at any time from the sidebar. Removing an item only affects your history list; anything you have already copied or published elsewhere is unaffected.
