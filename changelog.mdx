## Week of August 3, 2026

### Bug fixes

- **Brand profile limits are now enforced consistently.** Your plan's brand profile allowance is now checked whenever a new profile is created, so the cap you see in the app matches what's actually saved to your account. If you try to add a profile beyond your plan's allowance, you'll get a clear message explaining the limit and how to make room. Existing profiles above the new allowance are left in place — nothing you've already saved is removed. See [Plans & pricing](/plans/plans-and-pricing) for the allowance on each plan, or [Brand Profiles](/using/brand-profiles) for how to manage them.
