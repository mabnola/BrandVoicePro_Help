<Update label="August 2, 2026" description="This week">

## New features

- **Forgot password and self-serve password reset.** You can now recover your account from the sign-in screen. Click **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and we send a reset link. Locked-out accounts can also unlock themselves by completing a password reset. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) for sign-in help.
- **Change your password from your profile.** Update your password anytime from your account settings without needing to sign out first.

## Updates

- **Paid plans renamed to Basic and Premium.** The two paid tiers now use clearer names throughout the app, pricing page, and upgrade prompts. Nothing changes about your plan, price, limits, or billing — only the labels. Full details on [Plans & Pricing](/plans/plans-and-pricing).
- **Improved generation model consistency.** All content generations — articles, social posts, and SEO extras — now run on the same underlying model, so results are more consistent across features.

## Fixes

- Sign-in reliability improvements around password-based accounts, including clearer error messages when a password is incorrect or an account is temporarily locked.

</Update>
