<Update label="August 2, 2026">

## Fixes

**Extra protection when you change your account email**

Changing the email address on your BrandVoice Pro account is now safer:

- **Password required.** You must enter your current password to change the email on your account, matching the check that already existed for password changes.
- **Old address gets notified.** After a successful change, the previous email address receives a heads-up that the login identity moved, so you'd notice immediately if a change wasn't you.

If you ever get that notification and didn't make the change, reply to the message or reach out at [support@brandvoiceproapp.com](mailto:support@brandvoiceproapp.com).

No action needed — the new form in your profile will prompt you for the password the next time you update your email.

</Update>
