<Update label="August 2, 2026">

## Bug fixes

- **Word limits now apply to SEO title generation.** SEO titles are produced automatically at the end of each article personalization and are governed by the same per-plan word limit as article and social-post generation. Articles that exceed your plan's word limit will not run through SEO title generation. This makes limits consistent across all content types on [Free, Basic, and Premium](/plans/plans-and-pricing) plans.
- **SEO titles still do not count as a generation.** They are produced as part of the article you already ran, so your monthly [generation](/plans/generations-explained) allowance is not affected.

</Update>
