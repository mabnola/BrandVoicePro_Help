## Week of August 2, 2026

### Bug fixes

- **Sign-out now returns you to the top of the page.** Previously, if your session ended while you were scrolled down a long page — most noticeably after changing your password from your profile — the sign-in form would render offscreen above you. It now appears at the top of the viewport where you can see it.
