<Update label="August 2, 2026">

## Bug fixes

**More private sign-in errors.** When you enter an email that we do not recognize, the sign-in screen now returns a single, consistent "email not found" response with the option to [reset your password](https://app.brandvoiceproapp.com) or sign up. Previously, a rare legacy state could produce a different response for some addresses. The recovery flow is unchanged — if you have an older account, resetting your password still signs you in and re-links your account automatically.

Failed sign-ins are also slightly faster.

</Update>
