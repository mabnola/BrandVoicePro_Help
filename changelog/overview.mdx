<Update label="August 2, 2026">

## Bug fixes

**More private sign-in errors.** When you enter an email we do not recognize, the sign-in screen now returns a single, consistent "email not found" response. You still get the option to reset your password or sign up. Previously, a rare legacy state produced a different response for some addresses.

The recovery flow is unchanged — if you have an older account, resetting your password still signs you in and re-links your account automatically. Failed sign-ins are also slightly faster.

</Update>
