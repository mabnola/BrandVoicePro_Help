<Update label="August 2, 2026" tags={["Security", "Fixes"]}>
  ## Bug fixes

  - **Uploads now require sign-in.** Requests for upload URLs (used when you add a profile photo or brand asset) are now gated behind your account session, closing a gap where anonymous requests could be made. No action needed on your end — you're already signed in when uploading from your [profile](https://app.brandvoiceproapp.com) or [Brand Profiles](/using/brand-profiles).
