<Update label="August 2, 2026">

## Account security improvements

We tightened up how sessions and email-change notifications work, and made it easier to reach us when you can't sign in.

### Updates

- **Sessions end after credential changes.** Changing your password now signs you out on every device you were logged in on. Changing your email signs out every other device but keeps you signed in on the one you just used.
- **Contact Support button in emails.** Security-related emails (like password resets and email-change notices) now include a **Contact Support** button that opens a pre-addressed message to [support@brandvoiceproapp.com](mailto:support@brandvoiceproapp.com), with a plain-text address alongside it as a fallback.

### Fixes

- **Email-change notifications now send in both directions.** If you switched your account email from A to B and then back to A, the notification to the old address is no longer skipped. Every address change reliably notifies the previous email on file. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) if you need to reach support about your account.

</Update>
