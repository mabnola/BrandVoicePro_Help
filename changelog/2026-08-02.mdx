## Bug fixes

<Update label="August 2, 2026">
  **Content history is now strictly private.** Deleting an item from your content history now only affects your own account. Nothing about how you use [content history](/using/personalizing-content) changes on your side — your saved originals, personalized versions, and collateral are unchanged.

  If you delete an item in one browser tab and view your history in another, the list now refreshes to stay in sync.
</Update>
