## Updates

**Rate limits on sensitive actions**

To protect your account and keep the app responsive for everyone, a small number of actions are now capped per short time window:

- **Password reset** — up to 3 requests per 15 minutes for the same email address. Guards your inbox against reset-email flooding.
- **Sign in** — up to 10 attempts per 15 minutes for the same email address. Leaves ample room if you fumble your password, and cuts down on automated guessing.
- **SEO title generation** — up to 5 requests per minute per user. Prevents runaway usage while you iterate.

If you hit a limit, you'll see a message asking you to try again shortly. Normal use is unaffected.

See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) if you run into a "try again later" message unexpectedly.
